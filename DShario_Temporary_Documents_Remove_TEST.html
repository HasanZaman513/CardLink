How DShario works

A quick guide to sharing the right information safely.

1. Create your cardAdd the professional and contact information you want to use.
2. Choose a privacy viewProfessional, Business, Social or Full controls what the recipient can see.
3. Choose how to shareMy DShario Link is permanent and cannot be revoked. Temporary Share can last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days and can be revoked early.
4. Add a document if neededUpload a small PDF such as a CV and select it only for the secure share link that should include it.
5. Share and exchangeSend the link or QR code. The recipient can save your contact or send their contact back.

DShario Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

DShario is currently a prototype digital business card service. This policy explains how personal information is handled when you create an account, create a card, or use DShario.

1. Information we collect

Depending on what you choose to provide, DShario may collect your account email address and information for your digital card, including your name, job title, organisation, introduction, location, website, phone number, email address, WhatsApp number, social-media links, profile image, chosen card name and card appearance.

2. How we collect and use information

We collect information directly from you when you create an account or enter information into DShario. We use it to authenticate your account, create and store your card, display the information you choose to publish, provide sharing and download features, and operate and improve the prototype.

3. Public cards and your choices

DShario cards are designed for sharing. Information included on a public card may be viewed, copied, saved or shared by anyone who receives or discovers the card link. Do not add information you do not want to make public. You are responsible for ensuring that you have permission to publish information about another person.

DShario currently uses Supabase for account authentication and cloud storage/database services. The website is currently delivered using GitHub Pages. A third-party QR-code service may receive the public card URL when a QR code is generated. These providers may process technical information as part of providing their services.

5. Overseas processing

Some service providers used by DShario may process or store information outside Australia. Because this prototype may change its hosting configuration, DShario does not currently promise a particular overseas storage country. This policy will be updated when the production hosting and data locations are finalised.

We take reasonable steps appropriate to this prototype to protect account and card information. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and avoid placing sensitive information on a public card.

7. Access, correction and deletion

You can sign in to review or update your DShario card. The current prototype also provides a Delete Card function. If you need help accessing, correcting or deleting information associated with your account, use the contact method published on the DShario LinkedIn Page until a dedicated privacy contact address is established.

8. Retention

Card information is kept while it is needed to provide your DShario account and card, unless you delete your card or the information must be retained for legitimate operational or legal reasons. As the prototype develops, retention practices may be refined and this policy updated.

9. Children and sensitive information

DShario is intended for professional networking and is not designed for children. Please do not submit sensitive information such as health information, government identifiers, financial information or passwords as card content.

If you have a privacy question or complaint, contact DShario through its official LinkedIn Page. We will review the issue and respond within a reasonable time. A dedicated privacy email should be added before DShario moves beyond prototype testing.
