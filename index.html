How DShario works

A quick guide to sharing the right information safely.

1. Create your cardAdd the professional and contact information you want to use. Your Bangla name is optional; other information stays as you enter it.
2. Choose a privacy viewProfessional, Business, Social or Full controls what the recipient can see.
3. Choose how to shareMy DShario Link is your permanent card address. Temporary Share lasts 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days and can be revoked early.
4. Add PDFs to Temporary ShareUpload up to 2 PDFs, maximum 2 MB each. Remove or deselect a PDF before creating the share. Expiry or revocation stops future DShario access, but cannot remove a copy already downloaded by the recipient.
5. Share, QR and downloadSend a link or QR. You can also download a digital-card image; its QR returns to the live DShario card.
6. Exchange contactsA recipient can save your contact or send their own details back. Received Contacts is a temporary inbox: save useful contacts to the phone, then delete them from DShario.
7. English and BanglaThe language switch translates DShario interface and card labels. User-entered names, titles, companies, links and other content remain as entered.

DShario Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

DShario is currently a prototype digital identity and contact-sharing service. This policy describes the information handled by the current prototype and how it is used.

1. Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, DShario may handle account and authentication information; card information such as name, optional Bangla name, title, organisation, introduction, location, website, phone, email, WhatsApp, social links, profile image and card appearance; privacy-view and share-link settings; temporary-share tokens, expiry and revocation information; PDF documents you choose to upload for Temporary Share; and contact details voluntarily submitted through Contact Exchange.

2. Why we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, create and store cards, apply selected privacy views, generate permanent and temporary sharing experiences, provide QR and digital-card downloads, provide Temporary Share documents, deliver contact exchanges to the intended card owner, maintain basic service security and operate and improve the prototype.

3. Sharing and recipient access

DShario is designed for user-directed sharing. A card owner chooses what information to enter, which privacy view to use and whether to create a permanent or temporary share. Recipients may be able to view, copy, save, screenshot or forward information that has been shared with them. A temporary link's expiry or revocation prevents future access through DShario but cannot recall information or documents already downloaded or copied by a recipient.

4. Contact Exchange and Received Contacts

When a recipient chooses to send contact details back, those details are provided to the relevant card owner and stored in the owner's Received Contacts area subject to prototype limits. The owner can save a contact to their phone and delete it from DShario. DShario does not currently treat Received Contacts as a permanent address book.

5. Documents

PDF documents are optional and are available only through Temporary Share. Current prototype limits are displayed in the interface. Access through DShario follows the associated temporary link's validity. Users should avoid uploading highly sensitive documents and should understand that a recipient may retain a downloaded copy.

6. Service providers and storage

DShario currently uses Supabase for authentication and backend database/storage functions and uses website-hosting infrastructure to deliver the application. These providers may process technical and account/service information as necessary to provide their services. QR generation in the current DShario build is performed within the application rather than by sending the card URL to a separate QR-code service.

7. Overseas processing

Service providers may process or store information outside Australia or the user's country. Because DShario remains a prototype and its production infrastructure is not final, we do not currently promise a particular data-residency location. This section should be updated when production hosting and data locations are finalised.

8. Security

DShario uses access controls appropriate to the current prototype, including authenticated ownership controls and database access policies. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Use a strong unique password, choose privacy views carefully and avoid sharing highly sensitive information through a digital card.

9. Access, correction and deletion

Users can sign in to review and update their card, delete their card, and delete received contacts from the DShario inbox. Some account or backend information may require assistance to access or delete. Contact DShario through its official LinkedIn Page until a dedicated privacy contact is established. Deletion from DShario does not delete copies previously saved by another person.

10. Retention

Card and account-related information is retained while needed to provide the service or until deleted where the prototype provides deletion controls, subject to legitimate operational, security or legal needs. Temporary-share access follows its expiry or revocation status. Received Contacts is intended to be temporary rather than a permanent contact archive. Retention rules should be made more specific before commercial launch.

11. Language

The English/Bangla switch translates DShario interface text and card labels. Except for an optional alternate Bangla name, user-entered content is generally displayed as entered and is not represented as automatically translated.

12. Children and sensitive information

DShario is intended for professional and adult networking and is not designed for children. Do not use card fields or document sharing for passwords, payment credentials, government identifiers, health information or other highly sensitive information unless a future DShario feature expressly supports that category with appropriate safeguards.

13. Privacy questions and complaints

For a privacy question, correction/deletion request or complaint, contact DShario through its official LinkedIn Page. A dedicated privacy email and formal request process should be established before commercial launch.

14. Applicable privacy requirements

DShario is being developed in Australia and may be used in other countries. Privacy obligations can depend on where DShario operates and where users are located. The policy and operational practices must be reviewed against applicable law before commercial launch in each target market.

15. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as DShario's features, providers and information-handling practices change. The current version and update date will be published here.

Important: This is a prototype privacy policy, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
